# 三联关键帧母图（官方第 5 步）

Generate ONE 16:9 keyframe master image. Inside that single landscape frame, place THREE equal vertical 9:16 sub-keyframes side by side, left / center / right, with no gaps that read as a fourth scene. This is a continuity storyboard board, not a finished 9:16 video frame and not a filmstrip that will be interpolated as one wide shot.

LEFT panel = spatial establishment / start frame: lock room geometry, where each person stands, who fills the vertical 9:16 frame, eyeline and depth. Frozen moment, no motion blur.

CENTER panel = the main action of this beat at a later instant. Same people, costumes, faces, props, and location. Clearly different composition and visual focus from the left panel.

RIGHT panel = result or emotional resolution: the landing frame of the same shot. Same identities and spatial continuity, a distinct payoff, not a near-identical close-up of the left or center panel.

Use attached character cards as the only identity source. Use the attached scene card for world / layout / lighting. Match wardrobe, architecture, and recurring objects. Do not invent a new room or swap faces.

Live-action photographic realism only: grounded cinematography / continuity-photo language, stable human anatomy, realistic clothing physics, natural skin texture, pores, fine hair, physically plausible folds, correct lens perspective, restrained grading. Natural expression range. No illustration, toy-model, waxy skin, poster-art, anime, CG beauty render, or plastic symmetry.

STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: captions, names, arrows, panel numbers, speech bubbles, subtitles, watermarks, readable UI, split-screen inside a panel, letterboxing, beauty-filter look.
